## Refinement

The faceted bowl becomes a smooth curved bowl. The stem foot, top-left corner and inner counter are rounded. Curved top and side surfaces retain the original `(16, -12)` extrusion. The highlight follows the rounded bowl and uses round caps.

All five cubes use one rounded outer silhouette each, with blue faces joined beneath the silhouette to retain solid depth. Their original positions, sizes, 64-unit trail offset and fading opacities (`1`, `1`, `.72`, `.54`, `.3`) are retained.

The main front remains within x = 50–178, y = 44–231. The long stem, black stage (`#03070D`), cyan highlights (`#82F7FF`), electric-blue fronts (`#00D9F5`), deep-blue sides (`#008BBA`) and ProductIVF wordmark placement carry forward from V5. Additional blue gradient shades soften the extrusion.

Native SVG refinement; earlier versions and application code remain unchanged.
