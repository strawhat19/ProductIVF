# ProductIVF logo concepts — V2

Two refinements of V1's Silver facet and Task cells, using bright neon cyan-blue and black. Each family includes a standalone P, a large P paired with smaller IVF letters, and a four-letter PIVF composition.

## Concepts

Open `07-comparison.svg` or `07-comparison.png` to review all six variants together.

| Direction | Standalone P | P + IVF | PIVF |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neon facet | `01-neon-facet-p.svg` | `02-neon-facet-p-ivf.svg` | `03-neon-facet-pivf.svg` |
| Neon fused | `04-neon-fused-p.svg` | `05-neon-fused-p-ivf.svg` | `06-neon-fused-pivf.svg` |

**Neon facet** carries forward the dimensional idea from V1's Silver facet. Its continuous charcoal face has luminous cyan edges and subtle depth.

**Neon fused** develops V1's Task cells into one smooth, continuous blue letter. Its modular character appears only in the small pieces coming away from the very end of the stem.

## Shared changes

- A longer P stem stays solid below the bowl.
- The bowl and stem have no cell seams or detached sections.
- Only three small blocks separate at the terminal tip, with decreasing size and opacity.
- Vivid cyan-blue light and glow replace the pale blue and silver palette.
- The P + IVF versions give the P visual priority; the PIVF versions present all four letters together.
- Lettering is built from editable vector shapes.

## Reference and palette

The [second slide of the supplied Daniel Snows Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/BupFS37hQaw/?hl=en&img_index=2) was inspected in Chrome. Its dark interface and luminous cyan blocks guide this round's color and lighting.

Chosen palette:

- Neon cyan-blue: `#00D9F5`, `#00EDFF`.
- Bright highlights: `#82F7FF`.
- Blue depth: `#008BBA`.
- Black and charcoal: `#03070D`, `#11161E`.

The SVGs include dark presentation backgrounds so the glow is visible. The artwork, glow filters, and backgrounds remain editable. No source artwork from Instagram is embedded.
