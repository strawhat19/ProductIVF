# ProductIVF logo concepts — V1

Three directions for a **P whose lower leg separates into floating, fading blocks**. All artwork is editable SVG with a transparent canvas. Each direction includes a horizontal wordmark and a matching standalone icon.

| Direction | Lockup | Icon | Idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dissolving stem | `01-dissolving-stem.svg` | `01-dissolving-stem-icon.svg` | A smooth, continuous light blue P turns into blue, graphite and silver squares down its lower stem. The quietest, most compact direction. |
| Silver facet | `02-silver-facet.svg` | `02-silver-facet-icon.svg` | An angular silver P has a pale blue extruded edge. Its lower leg releases floating cubes with distinct lit top, front and side faces. |
| Task cells | `03-task-cells.svg` | `03-task-cells-icon.svg` | Separate task-sized cells construct the entire P. The bottom rows drift apart, connecting the visual idea to ProductIVF's lists, tasks and boards. |

## Palette

- Light blue: `#B5E3FA`, `#9AD5F5`, `#7CBFE5`, `#71B7DF`.
- Black / graphite: `#222C35`, `#34434F`, `#414C57`.
- Gray / silver: `#EFF3F6`, `#C2CCD5`, `#8E9BA8`, `#A7B4BF`.
- The floating pieces decrease in size and opacity toward the bottom of each stem. Their separation is intentional; the bowl stays solid or tightly organized so the P remains legible.

## Reference and construction

The user-provided [Daniel Snows Instagram reference](https://www.instagram.com/p/BupFS37hQaw/?hl=en&img_index=1) was viewed in Chrome. Its muted blue background, softly lit dimensional blocks and restrained fading informed the material and spacing choices, especially Silver facet. These are original P constructions; no artwork from the post is embedded or traced.

The horizontal lockups use an editable `ProductIVF` wordmark with an Inter / Avenir Next / Arial fallback stack. Text is live, so exact glyphs depend on locally installed fonts. For a production handoff, choose the font and convert the final wordmark to paths. Icons contain no font dependency.

The current wordmarks are intended for light backgrounds. The transparent icons can also be explored on dark surfaces; the smallest low-opacity blocks are decorative and may disappear at favicon sizes. An eventual small-size variant should retain the P and only the first detached row.
