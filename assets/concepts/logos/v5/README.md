## Changes from V4

- **Flat P:** narrows the lower stem from 48 to 36 units (`x=48–84`) while preserving the bowl, counter and tail endpoint at `y=231`. All seven detached blocks now use electric blue `#00D9F5`; their original positions, dimensions and fading opacities remain.
- **Faceted P:** all five detached cubes use cyan tops `#82F7FF`, electric-blue fronts `#00D9F5` and deep-blue sides `#008BBA`. The second cube's charcoal and black faces are blue. The main P, including its stem width, extrusion, shading and counter, is unchanged.
- Both directions retain the dark stage `#03070D`, the original neon gradients, 0.8-unit glow, long tails, block arrangement and canvas sizes. Full logos keep the original ProductIVF lettering and placement.

The full logos suit headers; the standalone marks suit avatars and large app-icon explorations. `03-comparison.svg` is the editable two-direction sheet, and `03-comparison.png` is its 2000 × 1220 preview. These are native SVG refinements; V4 and application code are preserved.
