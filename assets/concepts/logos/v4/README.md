## Tail adjustment

- Each continuous lower stem ends at `y=231`, extended by 64 units from V3's `y=167`.
- Each complete falling-block group moves down by 64 units. Block counts, individual geometry, relative positions, gaps and fading opacities are retained.
- The facet's lower side extrusion extends to the new stem tip: its front lower corner is `(96,231)` and its rear lower corner is `(112,219)`.
- Icon canvases grow from `256 × 304` to `256 × 368`; the marks retain their original scale.
- Full logo canvases grow from `920 × 320` to `920 × 384`. The wordmark baseline moves from `y=171` to `y=203`, maintaining its alignment as the mark grows taller.

The V3 bowls, faces, counter shapes, highlights, gradient coordinates and stops, black backgrounds, charcoal faces and 0.8-unit glow are retained. The full logos preserve the original live lettering, font fallback stack, font sizes and colors. The background rectangles are named `productivf-v4-background`.

`03-comparison.svg` and `03-comparison.png` present the two V4 icons together. The SVG is a self-contained native vector composition; the PNG is its rendered preview.
